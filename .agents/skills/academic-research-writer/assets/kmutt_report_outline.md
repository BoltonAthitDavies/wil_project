it; do not reorder, and omit a part only where noted.

Section numbering is `chapter.section.subsection`, and tables and figures are
numbered `chapter.sequence` in two independent series.

This project's realisation of the outline, file by file, is
`docs/report/final_report/main.tex`. Its Chapter 1 is INTRODUCTION, Chapter 2
theory and related work, Chapter 3 methodology, Chapter 4 experiments and
results, Chapter 5 conclusion; it has no appendix.
